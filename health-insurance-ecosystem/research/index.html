Health Insurance Ecosystem — Research Hub

Three 10-tab pages from one corpus and one voice router, researched 2026-09-25 ahead of Medicare AEP (Oct 15–Dec 7) and ACA open enrollment (Nov 1–Jan 15). The agent page is the business buyer; the Medicare and ACA pages are the consumers agents sell to. Every document belongs to exactly one page.

Independent Medicare & ACA agents

The business buyer: the agent or broker who buys leads, transfers and appointments.

3,137 buyer documents

  • Who: 3,137 first-person agent documents (3,020 health / general agents, 77 life or final-expense agents talking about buying leads, 40 new or aspiring agents) from r/InsuranceAgent, agent Facebook groups, Insurance-Forums.com and agent YouTube. Property & casualty agents, call-center staff, vendors and recruiters are excluded.
  • The lead is the whole job: Previous Solutions is the largest tab (320 documents bought leads from vendors, 86 aged leads, 104 transfers and inbound calls, 68 self-generated), and the top frustration after MLM recruiting is leads that are old, fake, shared or never answer (125 documents).
  • What they pay (their own words and the sellers' own ads): aged data $0.40–$7, shared leads $25–$50 split 3–5 ways, exclusive Medicare leads $35–$75 (more in AEP), Medicare inbound calls from $15.99 to $120+, final-expense transfers about $41–$43 in packs; the Lead Sources tab lists every priced sentence with its link.
  • What an enrollment is worth to them: $725 for a new Medicare Advantage enrollment in 2027 (CMS maximum), $363 a year after; ACA pays roughly $20–$30 per member per month. At exclusive-lead conversion that caps a Medicare lead at about $40 on first-year commission (Metrics tab).

Medicare shoppers (AEP)

The person turning 65 or already on Medicare, and the family helping. Buyer and user are the same person.

5,855 buyer documents

  • Who: 5,855 first-person Medicare shoppers (5,711 turning 65 or on Medicare, 144 family members helping), mostly commenters on Medicare explainer channels and r/medicare. Agents, SHIP counselors and former brokers are excluded.
  • What they fear: cost on a fixed income (68 documents), networks and prior authorization (44), a big illness and its bills (25). The whole decision is Original Medicare + Medigap versus Medicare Advantage.
  • What they tried: Plan G or N (82), agents and brokers (36), switching from Advantage back to Medigap (15); the most-liked outcome is relief at NOT choosing Advantage (155 peace-of-mind documents vs 119 happy on Advantage).
  • Who sells to them: 693 active Meta ads to Medicare shoppers, 243 from lead funnels and third-party marketers; benefit bait (giveback, flex card, grocery money, $0) is the most common hook after the 'Turning 65?' qualifier.

ACA marketplace shoppers (OEP)

The individual or family buying Obamacare coverage. Buyer and user are the same person.

1,309 buyer documents

  • Who: 1,309 first-person marketplace shoppers (r/HealthInsurance, r/Obamacare, r/personalfinance, early-retirement and self-employed YouTube). The voice is thinner than the Medicare page: marketplace buyers post less, and political comments without a personal plan detail are excluded.
  • What they fear: getting sick and not being able to pay (53 documents), the premium they will see this year (28), and the income math that decides the subsidy (89 documents manage their income to keep it).
  • What changed: the enhanced premium tax credits expired for 2026; CMS reports 23.1M plan selections (down 4.9%) and ~1.5M improper enrollments removed. The complaint ledger is led by premiums that doubled or tripled (57 documents).
  • Who sells to them: 414 active Meta ads to ACA shoppers; '$0 / low-cost coverage' is the leading hook.
