Finding help shouldn’t

Let’s talk about care
01

A conversation

Tell us what’s getting hard. What Mom wants. What you’re carrying. You don’t need to know the name of a service.

02

A plan in plain language

We explain the help, schedule, costs, and minimum hours. You have time to consider it, together.

03

A hello before care

You and Mom meet the caregiver before regular visits begin. If the fit feels wrong, tell us.

04

A gentle start

You can join the introduction. Where appropriate, we begin with something familiar, like lunch or companionship.

05

Someone stays on it

Your coordinator manages changes and keeps you informed. Care Notes help you know how the day went.

The Pink Glove Care Circle™

You shouldn’t
have to manage
the help.

Thoughtful care includes the things
you shouldn’t have to chase.

You hear from us first.

If a caregiver is delayed, unavailable, or a schedule needs attention, our job is to communicate first. You shouldn’t discover the problem when nobody shows up.

A change has someone on it.

Your coordinator works on the next step, explains the options, and keeps you informed. You know what’s happening and who is handling it.

The costs are clear.

The rate. The schedule. Any minimum hours or extra fees. What happens if plans change. Explained before you agree to care.

Mom still gets a say.

How she starts her morning. What she likes for lunch. What she wants to keep doing herself. Her preferences belong in the plan.
