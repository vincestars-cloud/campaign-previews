A few things you
might be wondering.

What does care cost?

It depends on the hours and the help she needs, and we will not pretend otherwise on a web page. You get the rate, the minimum and every additional fee in writing before you agree to anything.

What if Mom says she doesn’t want help?

That is the normal answer. Care can start with something that does not feel like care: a meal, a ride, or company for an afternoon. She meets the caregiver first.

How quickly can someone start?

Tell us the date you are working toward, including a hospital discharge. We will tell you on the first call what we can genuinely staff and by when.

Will the same caregiver come each time?

That is what we build the schedule around. When your regular caregiver is out, you hear it from us first, and we tell you who is coming instead.

Will I start getting calls from other agencies?

No. Your request comes to Pink Glove Care. We do not sell it and we do not pass it to a network.
