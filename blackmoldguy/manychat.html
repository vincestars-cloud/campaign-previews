"I already had someone look at it" →
Gently raise the conflict of interest: "One thing worth knowing — some companies that inspect also remediate, which creates a financial incentive to find problems. We keep those two services completely separate. You get an honest finding, and you decide what to do with it."

"My doctor says it's just allergies" →
"That's really common — mold symptoms are frequently misdiagnosed as allergies or sinus issues, sometimes for years. Testing is the only way to know for certain. An air test gives you a real answer instead of guessing."

We serve Metro Atlanta and Miami-Dade, Florida.

"What is black mold?" →
Stachybotrys chartarum is the mold people commonly call "black mold." It produces mycotoxins that can cause respiratory symptoms, brain fog, fatigue, and chronic illness. But other mold types cause similar effects — which is why lab testing matters more than visual identification.
