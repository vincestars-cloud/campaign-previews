## Pattern read
- Old proven statics (2–7 years) are stock photos with the brand name as headline: awareness ads franchisors keep on to hold the name. They tell us nothing about hooks, only that "hands" and "daughter hugging mom" have never been switched off.
- The only creative built around the daughter's actual language is Home Instead's UGC ("not a bad daughter", "the distance was killing me", "you don't have to do this alone") and it is the newest and most versioned. Every phrase in it appears verbatim in the forum corpus.
- Client-testimonial video (Angels There For You) resolves the top relationship fear in the corpus: a stranger in the house → one person you click with. Note it is a single 90-second static shot and still ran 215 days.
- Nobody in the proven tier sells: same caregiver every time, no 4-hour minimum, what to do the week after discharge, or how to pay. Those are the four gaps the forums scream about.
