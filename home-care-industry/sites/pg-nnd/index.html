What is home care?

Home care is non-medical help at home: personal care, companionship, meals, medication reminders, respite for family and support for people living with dementia. It lets older adults stay in their own home safely.

How does in-home care work?

You talk with a care advisor, we build a care plan around your loved one's routine, and we match her with a caregiver you meet before care begins. Visits can be a few hours a week or around the clock.

What services do you provide?

Personal care, companion care, respite care, Alzheimer's and dementia care, 24-hour and overnight care, hospital-to-home care, hospice support, and errands and transportation.

Is home care only for people who need a lot of help?

No. Many families start with a few hours a week for company, meals or rides, and add hours as needs change.

What is the difference between home care and home health care?

Home care is non-medical support with daily living. Home health care is skilled nursing or therapy ordered by a doctor. Many families use both.

How much does home care cost?

It depends on the hours and the level of care your loved one needs. There is no minimum number of hours. A care advisor will walk you through it in a free consultation.

Can I choose my caregiver?

Yes. We match your loved one with a caregiver based on her needs and personality, and you meet before care begins. If it is not the right fit, we will find one who is.

Do you do background checks?

Yes. Every caregiver is background-checked, reference-checked, trained, bonded and insured.

How quickly can care begin?

Often within a few days of your first call, and sooner when the situation calls for it. Ask your care advisor for a start date on the call.

Let's talk about her care.

We wear the gloves so you can be family. Our caregivers handle the hands-on care, kindly and on your schedule, so the time you have together is yours.
