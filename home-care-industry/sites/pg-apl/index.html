The questions she asks us.

How much does it cost?

We are private pay, by the hour, with no minimum. You will hear the hourly rate in the first two minutes of the call, before we ask you anything else.

Can I meet and choose the caregiver?

Yes. You meet her before the first visit. If the person who arrives is not the one you met, that visit is free.

What if Mom refuses?

Most do at first. The first visit is a cup of coffee, not a stranger with a badge. We will tell you what has worked for other families before we come.

What if the caregiver doesn't show?

You hear from us before the shift, not after, and the backup you have met comes instead. A missed visit is on us.

Do you take Medicaid?

No. We are private pay. If Medicaid is your situation, we will give you the Georgia programs and the Area Agency on Aging line that can actually help, instead of a voicemail.

Will Medicare pay for this?

No. Medicare does not cover this kind of care at home. Our funding guide covers what does: long-term care insurance, VA Aid and Attendance, and Georgia's waiver programs.

Will it be the same person every time?

Yes. Same caregiver, every visit, plus a named backup you have also met.

How fast can you start?

Tell us what is happening and we will give you a real date on the call, not a promise we cannot keep.

Do I sign a contract?

No. You are billed only for hours delivered, you can cancel with one call, and you get a dated notice before any charge.
