A few questions you may have

Let’s make room
for the questions.

What if Mom doesn’t want a caregiver?+

Start with what she would welcome: help with a meal, company on a walk, or support with one tiring task. Give her time to ask questions and a say in the introduction.

What if her memory or care needs are changing?+

Tell us what you’re noticing and what support is already in place. The next conversation should clarify the level of care needed and whether a different provider or service is appropriate.

What about evenings or more regular support?+

Bring the schedule you need to the conversation. The provider should confirm availability, the scope of care, and how changes or missed visits are handled before you begin.
